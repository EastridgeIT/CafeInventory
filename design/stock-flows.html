Buying, checking in and moving stock

Working demo of the three new flows, with sample cafe items. Everything shares one set of numbers: buy something and watch Undelivered grow, put it away and watch it land on shelves, then rebalance. The table at the bottom updates live and shows that only a purchase changes the total.

  1. 1 · Shopping listTap an item, enter how many you bought. It goes straight into stock as Undelivered.
  2. 2 · Check inWhen it arrives, split it across places: 8 here, 12 there. Leftover stays undelivered.
  3. 3 · RebalanceSet the new quantity for one place. The difference comes from (or goes to) another place. The total never changes.
