Shelf map

Admin view of one location, laid out the way the room is built. Each shelf scrolls sideways. Try it with a mouse or your finger; the items are sample pantry stock.

  • Scrolling is the defaultSwipe or scroll any shelf freely. Nothing moves by accident. Use the arrows on a shelf's header with a mouse.
  • Tap Rearrange to change thingsThen drag a tile by its handle, or tap a tile to pick it up and tap a + gap to place it. Scrolling still works the whole time. Tap Done when finished.
  • Press and hold for "Move to"Anytime, hold a tile (or tap its three dots, or right-click) to pick a rack and shelf from a list, or nudge it left or right. Same location only.
