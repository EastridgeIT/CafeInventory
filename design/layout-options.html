Cafe Inventory layouts

Three directions for how volunteers count, how Kristyn shops, and how admins place items on shelves. Every screen below is a working demo with sample cafe items. Counting uses one tap where it can: number buttons sized to each item (try the milk, cups and bottled water), fullness buttons for the cream cheese case, and a keypad only where needed. Option A is the chosen foundation; B and C are kept for reference.
